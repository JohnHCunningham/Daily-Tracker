Last updated: May 28, 2026

## 1. CONNECTION REQUESTS (Outbound)

**Source:** Sales Navigator searches (ICP: VP Sales, Sales Managers, Enablement, CROs — 5-50 rep companies)
**Format:** 300 chars max, no subject line, one warm sentence
**Pattern:** "Hi [Name] — [personalized reason]. Would be great to connect."
**Two versions:** V-A (short/punchy), V-B (slightly longer/warmer)
**Batch:** CSV export → Evaboot → scoring → dedupe → draft → HTML form
**Daily target:** ~25-40 requests via Sales Nav

## 2. ACCEPTANCES → THANK-YOU

**Trigger:** Someone accepts connection request
**Timing:** Within 24 hours
**Format:** Short, warm, no pitch. One sentence.
**Pattern:** "[Name] — thanks for connecting. [Light personal touch]."
**Delivery:** HTML form with Copy buttons, paste into LinkedIn message
**Track:** LEDGER.md (daily acceptances count)

## 3. LAG TIMES — THE RHYTHM

LinkedIn punishes speed. The right delay signals confidence, not desperation.

| Action | Lag | Why |
|--------|-----|-----|
| Profile view → connection request | 3 days | Same-day looks hawkish. They forgot they clicked you. |
| Request sent → no response | 7 days | Withdraw stale requests, don't let them pile up |
| Acceptance → thank-you note | Within 24h | Warm but not immediate. Next morning is ideal. |
| Thank-you → second DM | NEVER | Second DM without engagement reads as a pitch |
| Thank-you → comment on their post | 3+ days | Don't comment the day after thanking — too eager |
| Their comment on YOUR post → reply | 2-4 hours | Let conversation breathe, don't pounce |
| Their reply to your comment → next comment | Same rhythm | Don't turn their post into your thread |
| Comment exchange ×2 → DM check-in | 2+ weeks | "Hey, enjoyed your thinking on X. Would love to connect further." |
| DM conversation → discovery call mention | After 2-3 DM exchanges | Only if they show buying signals |

**Red flags that you moved too fast:**
- They liked your comment but didn't reply
- One-word replies to your DM
- They viewed your profile after your comment but didn't engage

## 4. PROFILE VIEWS — READING THE SIGNAL

Not all profile views are equal. The source tells you intent.

**Types of profile viewers:**

| Source | Intent | Priority |
|--------|--------|----------|
| Clicked your post → viewed profile | Content resonated. Wants to know who you are. | HIGH |
| Sales Nav search result | Looking for someone LIKE you. Might be researching competitors. | MEDIUM |
| Comment on someone else's post | They saw your thinking and got curious. | HIGH |
| Mutual connection's network | Browsing, not hunting. | LOW |
| Random / unknown source | LinkedIn algorithm suggested you. | LOW |

**What to do:**
1. Check Sales Nav viewers list every morning **AFTER the acceptance report**
2. Cross-reference viewers against today's acceptance list — if they already accepted, skip. They got a thank-you, not a connection request.
3. Note the source if visible
4. Wait 3 days from view date
5. Send connection note: reference what they likely saw ("Enjoyed your thinking on..." if they came from a post comment)
6. Track: profile viewers convert 2-3x higher than cold 2nd-degree

**Critical sequencing rule:** Profile viewer outreach happens AFTER acceptance processing. Sending a connection note to someone who already accepted your request looks like you're not paying attention. The LEDGER acceptance numbers come first, then profile viewers.

## 5. ONGOING RELATIONSHIP DEVELOPMENT

The long game. Most connections go nowhere because nobody tends the garden.

**Phase 1: The Handshake (Week 1)**
- Connection accepted → thank-you note within 24h
- Do nothing else. Let them see your name, then your content.

**Phase 2: The Feed Presence (Weeks 1-4)**
- Your Tue/Wed/Thu posts appear in their feed
- They may or may not engage — that's fine
- You're building pattern recognition: "I see this guy regularly"

**Phase 3: The Comment Exchange (Ongoing)**
- When they post in your wheelhouse, leave a thoughtful comment
- Goal: 2-3 comment exchanges over several weeks
- Each exchange builds a thread of shared thinking
- Now you're not a stranger — you're someone whose thinking they recognize

**Phase 4: The DM Re-engagement (Week 4+)**
- Only after 2-3 real comment exchanges AND 2+ weeks since thank-you
- Open with: "Really enjoyed your take on X the other day. Got me thinking about Y."
- Still no pitch. This is peer-to-peer.
- If they engage, let the conversation run

**Phase 5: The Call (When They Signal)**
- They ask what you do
- They mention a problem OCC solves
- They engage with 3+ of your posts in a short window
- THEN: "Happy to hop on a quick call if you ever want to compare notes on [topic]."
- NOT: "Let me show you my product."

**What kills relationships:**
- Pitching before Phase 4
- Second DM that's a brochure
- Commenting on every single post they make
- Asking for a call the moment they engage once

**The principle:** let them come to you. Your job is to be visible, thoughtful, and patient. The content and comments do the selling — you just show up.

## 6. COMMENT PROTOCOL

**When:** Someone in ICP posts in your wheelhouse (sales methodology, coaching, discovery, leadership)
**Format:**
1. Acknowledge with specificity — show you read it
2. Add one layer most people missed
3. End with open-ended question (not yes/no)
4. No mention of OCC. No pivot to what you do.
**Tone:** Respectful peer, not expert-on-high
**Volume:** Max 2 comments per person per session
**Non-connections who post:** High-value targets. Your comment IS the handshake. If they didn't accept your request but you comment thoughtfully on their post, they often connect after.

**Cadence:** 1 post per group per week (Tue/Wed/Thu)
**Groups:** Sandler Sales Training, The Challenger Sale, Linking Sales Leaders

**Tracking:** group-posting-log.md

## 10. TRACKING & INTEGRITY

**LEDGER.md** — Daily bookkeeper entry
  - Two inputs from Sales Nav each morning: total connections, new acceptances
  - Everything else calculated from those two numbers
  - Reconciled checkbox each day

**Daily counter** — Activity points (requests, comments, posts, connections)
  - Comprehensive 160-point system across Revenue Engine / Builder / Strategic
  - Counter corruption protocol: verify with LEDGER numbers
