1. Connection Requests

Source: Sales Navigator — VP Sales, Sales Managers, Enablement, CROs at 5–50 rep companies

Format: 300 chars max, no subject line, one warm sentence

Pipeline: CSV → score → filter → dedupe → draft → HTML form

Target: 25–40 per day

🌱
4. Relationship Phases

Wk 1 Handshake — thank-you only. Let them see your name.

Wk 1–4 Feed Presence — content does the work. Pattern recognition.

Ongoing Comment Exchange — 2–3 thoughtful exchanges. Build shared thinking.

Wk 4+ DM Re-engage — only after real exchange. "Loved your take on X."

Signal The Call — they ask what you do OR engage 3+ posts. Then: "Happy to compare notes."

💬
5. Comment Protocol

3-part format:

① Acknowledge with specificity — show you read it

② Add one layer most people missed

③ End with open-ended question

Never: mention OCC, pitch, or pivot to what you do.

Max: 2 comments per person per session.

Non-connections: Your comment IS the handshake. Often converts better than a cold request.

7. InMail

Status: Paused (0 credits)

Pipeline: SCAN → SCORE → FILTER → DEDUPE → DRAFT → BUILD → REPORT

Reserved for high-value prospects only. Subject + body. Full length.

8. Tracking & Integrity

LEDGER.md — bookkeeper standard. Two numbers from Sales Nav each morning. Everything else calculated.

160-pt System — Revenue Engine (130) + Builder (50) + Strategic (30). Verified against LEDGER.

Daily sequence: ① Sales Nav numbers ② Acceptances ③ Thank-yous ④ Profile viewers ⑤ New requests ⑥ Close LEDGER
